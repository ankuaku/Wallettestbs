Telegram alerts

Get a Telegram message when someone connects a wallet, approves or activates billing, when a charge goes through or fails, or when something needs your attention. Billing keeps working automatically; alerts let you check everything by hand.

Connect your bot

  1. In Telegram, open @BotFather, send /newbot, and copy the token it gives you.
  2. Paste the token below and press Save.
  3. Open your new bot in Telegram and press Start (or add it to a group and send a message there).
  4. Press Find my chat, then Send test.
