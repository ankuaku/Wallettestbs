    • Your approval is the cap. One confirmation sets it up, and we can never collect more than the amount you approve.
    • Per charge (optional). A single bill can never be larger than the amount you choose.

    • Straight to us. Payments can only go to our treasury address, set in the contract.
    • Cancel any time. Cancel here, or revoke the USDT approval from your wallet, and no further charges can go through.
